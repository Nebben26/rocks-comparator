FAQ

Know exactly what you’re joining.

What exactly is The Battlemap?

A continuously maintained political and cultural intelligence product built around material-change detection, sourced relationships, watchlists, and persistent history.

Is this another news feed?

No. The product is organized around what changed, how it connects, and why it matters to your watchlist—not a chronological stream of every story.

What counts as a connection?

A defined factual relationship such as ownership, employment, funding, lobbying, partnership, appointment, litigation, or another precise predicate backed by a source.

Does a documented relationship mean wrongdoing?

No. A documented relationship is simply a documented relationship. Analysis, motive claims, disputes, and unresolved questions remain separate.

What does membership cost?

The planned membership is $99 per month. The current form only requests access; it does not collect payment or begin a subscription.
